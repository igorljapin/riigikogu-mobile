# November 2026 data review

The automated snapshot is dated November 1. The following corrections were
checked against official English member profiles on November 9, 2026:

- [Diana Ingerainen](https://www.riigikogu.ee/en/parliament-of-estonia/members-of-the-riigikogu/8946b072-80c5-436e-95b2-41f928a7c636/diana-ingerainen/)
  is deputy chair of the Estonia 200 Parliamentary Group.
- [Kadri Tali](https://www.riigikogu.ee/en/parliament-of-estonia/members-of-the-riigikogu/2bc608c1-1c67-4db8-968b-94bc3947dfac/kadri-tali/)
  chairs European Union Affairs and remains in the Estonia 200 Parliamentary Group.
- [Peeter Tali](https://www.riigikogu.ee/en/parliament-of-estonia/members-of-the-riigikogu/b6f8dff5-7c69-4d4c-b978-be0a2ad6e161/peeter-tali/)
  remains a member of European Union Affairs and chairs National Defence.

The automated snapshot omitted Peeter's ordinary EU Affairs membership when
removing his former chairmanship. His membership was restored in `data/mps.json`
from the official profile; the committee resolver has not been changed to infer
ongoing membership from expired records. The published-data regression test
will flag a subsequent refresh that omits it again. Recheck the official source
before changing that expectation if membership later changes.

Ended role records for Diana and Kadri were also being reported as departures
from their still-current parliamentary group. `left_faction` now excludes ended
records for the current group, while retaining departures from other groups.
Their incorrect `leftFaction` and `leftFactionDate` values were cleared.

Registered and voting-bloc totals, alignment and seating are unchanged. The
profile URLs merged in PR #57 are retained for all 101 members. Service-worker
cache v11 delivers the refreshed data and Andres Sutt's updated portrait to
returning users.
